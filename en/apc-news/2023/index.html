A Popular Science AI for APC Readers Is in Training

The APChat language model is intended to give readers a faster way to look up popular science information and offer more ways to learn through the APC Library. JJ and Shiguang built the model, and APC’s science writers supplied its training dataset. Based on Baichuan-13B, it was trained with QLoRA-8B quantized fine-tuning and SFT instruction tuning. APChat is still in internal testing, so stay tuned!

Scientist Cosplay No. 7: A Fan Interpretation of Su Song

My Roommate Is from a Thousand Years Ago

A Su Song fan shoot based on my own interpretation of Entering the Unscientific
Su Song, courtesy name Zirong, was born in Nan’an, Quanzhou, during the Northern Song dynasty. His work encompassed politics, astronomy, mechanical engineering, science, medicine, diplomacy, and literature. He served as chancellor and achieved 7 world firsts. In his own era, Su Song was a true polymath. A BBC documentary once observed that rather than calling Su Song the Leonardo da Vinci of the East, it would be more fitting to call Leonardo the Su Song of the West.

From steamed buns and bread to industry’s “three acids and two alkalis,” the Hou process is everywhere. That unassuming white powder laid a foundation for an entire nation’s chemical industry. “Why did you choose to become a naval astronomer?”
“There were many reasons, really. But the stars are there, aren’t they?”
This conversation may never have taken place, and Messier’s answer was probably different. Even so, I believe it expresses something Messier and many astronomy enthusiasts have felt.
