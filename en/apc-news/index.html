The 2019 Gewu Annual: Preorders Are Open

The 2019 Gewu Annual: a popular science yearbook for everyone who loves science

Do you find yourself drawn to the night sky, only to be put off by dense astronomical terminology? Do you want to introduce a child to science, but worry that short online videos may be full of errors and teach the wrong ideas?

Then this long-delayed popular science annual was made for you. It brings together outstanding articles by writers and reviewers from APC’s science communication team, written with honesty and clarity to reveal both the hard facts and the extraordinary beauty of our world.

Many readers may ask: Why are we publishing a 2019 annual in 2026? Will the articles be out of date?

The answer is simple and heartfelt. The year 2019 marked the beginning of our society’s work in science communication, while 2026 is Gewu Society’s tenth anniversary. These excellent articles should not remain buried in old archives; they deserve to reach readers. Knowledge itself does not expire, and we have also reviewed every article carefully to ensure that its content remains current. The editorial team of APC’s science communication group promises that annuals for other years will follow the 2019 edition. Stay tuned!

What will you find in the 2019 Gewu Annual?

You can follow some of the universe’s greatest stories, from the origin of the solar system to the Opportunity rover’s 15-year journey. We will help you understand the details of the stars above, making astronomy feel less remote or inaccessible.

You can also discover the science hidden in everyday life. Why can a Rubik’s Cube no longer be solved after a single corner is twisted? What kind of “magic” does GPS use to keep us from getting lost? What happens to a typhoon from birth to dissipation? How does AI simulate human intelligence? Across mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, computer science, and Earth science, we unpack familiar experiences through clear logic and memorable analogies. The result is rigorous science that remains readable, understandable, and easy to remember, even for complete beginners.

You will also see science and the humanities meet: the stars hidden in classical poetry, a soothing field journal about birds on campus, illustrated guides to plants, and deeper reflections on the meaning of life, the purpose of education, and the future imagined by science fiction. The annual does more than explain what something is; it asks why. Alongside careful scientific research, it captures a group of young people’s sincere love of science and the ideals that drive them.

The publication contains no commercial advertising or clickbait. Every article has been checked repeatedly, and every dataset comes from an authoritative source. It can serve as a child’s first introduction to science or as a rewarding read for adults who want to fill gaps in their knowledge and make room for curiosity.

Knowledge begins with investigating things; everything in the world has an underlying order.

We hope opening this annual will also open a new way of looking at the world.
