Science Communication and Debate Competition: Season 1

APC will hold Season 1 of its Science Communication and Debate Competition from 8/3 to 10/15, with generous prizes. Every Union member may take part. Contests are held in this group, and members of outside organizations may enter as organized teams. An administrator must approve the content of every session. Register with administrator A3.
Science communication:

  1. Contestants should prepare their own science communication script. The subject may be natural science, philosophy, or any other positive, rule-compliant topic, and administrator A3 must review it. Presentations may take place in this group each day from 8:00 to 10:00, 13:00 to 15:00, or 18:00 to 22:00. No more than three contestants may present per day. Group members will score each presentation by vote under real-time administrative supervision. After a presentation, up to 10 audience members may ask the contestant as many as 30 questions, provided they are not unreasonably difficult; answering is optional. The highest-rated contestant will be selected at the end of the season.
  2. Contestants may instead write a science article or produce a video and upload it to the group for members to score.

Debate: Contestants should form affirmative and negative teams, agree on a motion, and submit it to an administrator for review under the same content rules as the science communication contest. No debate may have more than 8 participants, and neither side may have more than 4. Members may select their own moderator. Members will score the debate afterward, and contestants may enter more than once.

Winter Break Activity Plan

Hallo, everyone! APC-kun here.
As the fourth leadership term comes to a close, we have a wide range of winter-break activities for everyone. Join us! [There are lovely prizes, so do not miss out!!]

Event 1: Physics Postcard Submissions

Remember the chemistry postcard submissions for the Union’s first anniversary? We selected outstanding entries and turned them into postcards. This time, physics enthusiasts can show their writing skills too! Send us your own expression of what physics means to you. We will turn every submission into a postcard, and each participant will receive one copy free. Other friends are welcome to buy them as well.

Event 2: Lunar New Year Subject Mascot Design Competition

Ta-da! The Lunar New Year is almost here, and the long-awaited winter break is approaching. APC is seeking mascot designs for five subjects: astronomy, physics, chemistry, mathematics, and biology. You may draw only one or create designs for all five.
After a vote, the design with the most votes will become APC’s mascot for 2018!

More activities are being planned. Follow APC-kun for updates!
