Congratulations to an APC Member!

Great news!
TC-RAG, the latest research involving a Gewu Society member, achieved a score of 87.95 on medical postgraduate entrance-exam questions, making it the strongest Chinese-language medical model currently available.
TC-RAG uses a stack to simulate an LLM’s memory, allowing information to be pushed and popped. It is the first Turing-complete RAG system. Two meta-actions, push and pop, simulate a Turing machine and can be combined into more complex actions: summarization pops and then pushes, reflection pops, and tool observation pushes. This helps the language model use RAG more effectively and manage its memory more efficiently.

Congratulations to Two APC Members!

Happy Lunar New Year! A-kun has more good news to share as the new year begins.
Zhang Wentao and Jiang Xinke, APC members at Wuxi Smart Deep Insight Technology Co., Ltd., have worked with researchers from MIT, University College London, the Chinese Academy of Sciences, and other institutions on supply-chain management and allocation. Their Deep Black-Litterman Model (DBLM) brings a financial model into supply-chain management and uses spatiotemporal graph neural networks to optimize order-allocation strategies automatically, improving supply efficiency. The work has been submitted to a leading deep-learning conference, and the paper is available on arXiv. We look forward to advancing innovation in supply-chain management together with researchers and practitioners everywhere! /sparkle
Congratulations to both APC members!

Scientist Cosplay No. 8: Charles Robert Darwin (Indoor Shoot)

The Chronicles of Narnia: Tianjin Edition—Tales of the Hai River
“The stairs in my house lead straight to the Galápagos Islands. I can collect specimens without even going outside.”
“Well, imagine that! My front door opens onto the Hai River. I can jump off the balcony for a swim, then come back up and make Eight-Treasure Tofu!”
“Now that’s convenient cooking. But how do you get to work?”
“Work, shmerk. Just enjoy yourself. Speaking of which, how do you cook? There’s nothing at all where you live!”
“Cook? What is cooking?”
“Forget I asked. My mistake!”

I reshot Darwin mainly because I was not satisfied with the studio or characterization from '19. I entered the cosplay community in 2017 and finally completed this shoot in 2023. Somehow, 7 years have passed along the way. The Darwin shoot from 2019 still feels vivid, although some of the friends from that time have since disappeared into the crowd. I am 25 now. I have cosplayed every character I wanted to, and this year I hope to move into science communication. Wish me luck, and happy birthday to me. Thank you to everyone who provided props. Meeting people is a matter of fate; becoming friends is a blessing. I hope you enjoy this year’s finished set!
