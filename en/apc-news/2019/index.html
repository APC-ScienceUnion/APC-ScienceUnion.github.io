How to get the preliminary-round exam:
QQ: The APC Science Union main group and recruitment group will post an announcement with the exam link. If you have not joined the main group, you are welcome to join the APC recruitment exam group: 1027526691.
WeChat: Follow the WeChat official account “NOVA1670” or “APC Science Union” and reply with the keyword “competition” to receive the paper for free.

The final, whose date has also not been set, will use the same rules as the semifinal and take place in APC’s main QQ group. The top 8 semifinalists advance to the final.

Recruiting Science Communicators | APC's Recruitment Program Is Now Open

APC Union, also nicknamed the A-Pike Union and referred to below as “the Union,” is a student science organization founded on 2016/7/5. Based primarily on QQ, it now consists mostly of high school and university students. We work to create a positive online community where students with shared goals can move forward together, and we are expanding into science communication. An entrance exam helps maintain a student-centered group with a strong learning environment and high-quality discussion.

[Recruitment System]
We take member selection seriously. Every spring and summer, during the winter and summer breaks, we hold recruitment exams. For example, the online exam held during the 2017 winter break offered five subjects: biology, mathematics, physics, chemistry, and astronomy. Passing any one subject qualified an applicant to become a full member of the Union.
This July, we will again hold a recruitment exam to find new talent. This year’s six subjects are mathematics, chemistry, physics, astronomy, biology, and geography. You can choose the difficulty and structure of your paper, giving you more opportunities to show what you can do!

Orion’s Starry-Sky Chronicles follows Orion, an apprentice star mage, as he explores the constellations. In the comic, Orion takes the role of the protagonist in each constellation’s story and experiences its legends firsthand, learning about the constellations along the way.

The series explains constellations through astronomy rather than astrology, so it covers all 88 constellations in the sky, not only those of the zodiac.

Element Universe Project No. 1 Revealed

Element Universe, or EU, Project No. 1: A Few Details About Hydrogen

H was the first element to arrive in EU. She is actually older than everyone else but looks the youngest, so she also acts younger than anyone else.
As the adult of Group IA, or at least someone no one dares call anything else, she gets along well with every member of her group (probably?). In private, however, she prefers Group VIIA and seems to know many of F’s little secrets (??).
H is so light that she can float simply by standing still, and she moves very quickly. Do not try to play tag with her.
H seems to know many things the other elements do not, especially about… the Creator (see the EU setting) and events from the distant past. She may be hiding a plan no one else knows about.
When she attacks, flames burst from her fingertips. If her target is reactive enough, H may also hydrogenate it. Beyond that, she appears to retain energy left over from the creation of EU.
